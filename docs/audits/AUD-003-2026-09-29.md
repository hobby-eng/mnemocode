## Review evidence

### Scope and methodology

Reviewed MnemoCode 0.1.0 at the commit above: date handling and the Seedshift transformation, the five representations and the MNC1 record, the card renderers and the `mnemocode/cards` entry point that lets another program render the same cards, the input limits, the help text and the documents.

Method: the source of these parts was read, and the behaviour was then exercised with public test data only (`abandon … about`). Edge cases of the `mnemocode/cards` entry point were probed from a browser-style host that supplies its own random source, QR matrix and PNG coding. The longest built-in texts were rendered in every template to look for text that does not fit.

Not in scope: the Shamir share engine and its WASM build, the local Bitcoin recovery checks, image export through Poppler, the reproducible build, and platforms other than Linux x64 with Node.js 26.10.0.

No evidence folder is kept. The evidence is the commands below and the regression tests named in each finding.

### Findings

#### AUD-003-FUN001 — Medium — A date with a short year is read as a year of the first century

- **Category:** FUN.
- **Severity:** medium.
- **Status:** verified.
- **Release blocking:** false.
- **Affected files and builds:** `src/core/dates.ts`; every command that takes dates, and every program that uses the core.
- **Reproduction:** parse `23-09-26`, or `5-01-02`.
- **Expected behavior:** an error. The documents promise `DD-MM-YYYY` or `YYYY-MM-DD` with a four-digit year.
- **Observed behavior:** `23-09-26` was accepted as year 23, month 9, day 26, and `5-01-02` as year 5. The year of the second form could have one to four digits.
- **Impact:** a person who types a short year gets a shift that differs from the one for the intended date, with no warning. The phrase can still be restored with the same short spelling, but not with the full date the person remembers.
- **Evidence:** the probe output; `test/core.test.ts`, "needs a four-digit year in both spellings".
- **Recommended fix:** require four digits in both forms.
- **Required verification:** short years are refused; `0026` gives the year 26.

#### AUD-003-FUN002 — Low — Invented email and website addresses can belong to a real company

- **Category:** FUN.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Status:** verified.
- **Release blocking:** false.
- **Affected files and builds:** `src/export/card-identities.ts`, `src/export/card-settings.ts`; every business card with invented details.
- **Reproduction:** export business cards without `--card-email` and `--card-website`.
- **Expected behavior:** invented contacts are decoration and point to nobody.
- **Observed behavior:** the addresses had the form `contact@<company>.com` and `<company>.com`. Some of these domains are registered by real companies.
- **Impact:** a printed card could name the mailbox and website of a real company. No secret is involved. Details supplied by the person are printed as given.
- **Evidence:** source inspection; `test/card-session.test.ts`, `test/business-cards.test.ts`.
- **Recommended fix:** use the `.example` domain, which RFC 2606 reserves for examples.
- **Required verification:** every invented address ends in `.example`; supplied addresses are unchanged.

#### AUD-003-API001 — Low — A sheet size together with `--cards-dir` gives cards of the sheet size

- **Category:** API.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Status:** verified.
- **Release blocking:** false.
- **Affected files and builds:** `src/export/render.ts`, `src/export/sskr-content.ts`; `renderIndividualCards` of the `mnemocode/cards` entry point.
- **Reproduction:** ask for separate cards with the page size `a6`.
- **Expected behavior:** the page size decides the result: `wallet` and `business` give separate cards, `a6` and `a4` give one sheet. A request that contradicts this is refused.
- **Observed behavior:** eight separate cards were produced, each on a page of A6 size.
- **Impact:** an unexpected result that no document describes. No data is lost.
- **Evidence:** the probe output; `test/card-page-sizes.test.ts`, "refuses separate cards of a sheet size".
- **Recommended fix:** refuse separate cards for a sheet size. `--cards-dir` only names the folder for separate cards.
- **Required verification:** the request fails with a message that names the card sizes; a card size and no size still work.

#### AUD-003-API002 — Low — Renderer errors name command-line options and the wrong text

- **Category:** API.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Status:** verified.
- **Release blocking:** false.
- **Affected files and builds:** `src/export/render.ts`, `src/export/sskr-content.ts`, `src/export/collection-sheet.ts`.
- **Reproduction:** from another program, ask for a QR code on separate cards; or supply a name of 100 characters.
- **Expected behavior:** a message that every host can show and that names the text which does not fit.
- **Observed behavior:** the first message advised `--page-size` and `--card-qr`, which a program with a form does not have. The second said that the "Study heading" is too long, although the supplied text was the name.
- **Impact:** confusing messages outside the command line. No secret is involved.
- **Evidence:** the probe output; `test/card-page-sizes.test.ts`, `test/render-platform.test.ts`.
- **Recommended fix:** word the renderer messages without option names, and pass a label for the text being fitted.
- **Required verification:** no renderer message contains `--`; the message for a long text names that text.

#### AUD-003-ARC001 — Low — Page sizes, the millimetre unit and a colour conversion are duplicated

- **Category:** ARC.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Status:** verified.
- **Release blocking:** false.
- **Affected files and builds:** the layout and artwork modules under `src/export/`; `src/export/card-session.ts`.
- **Reproduction:** search for the page dimensions, the `MM` constant and the `hsv` function.
- **Expected behavior:** one definition of each.
- **Observed behavior:** the table of page dimensions existed in four files, `MM` in three and `hsv` in two. The card session had one line that could never run.
- **Impact:** a change of a page size must be repeated in four places; a missed place gives pages of different sizes.
- **Evidence:** search result; `test/card-page-sizes.test.ts`, "knows the size of every page in both orientations".
- **Recommended fix:** `pageDimensions` in `card-settings.ts`, one `MM`, a shared `color-math.ts`; replace the dead line with an explicit check.

#### AUD-003-ARC002 — Low — The compact sheet layout cannot be reached through the program

- **Category:** ARC.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Status:** withdrawn.
- **Release blocking:** false.
- **Affected files and builds:** `src/export/collection-sheet.ts`.
- **Reproduction:** follow the callers of `collectionSheetLayout` for the `wallet` and `business` sizes.
- **Expected behavior:** code that the program can reach, or no such code.
- **Observed behavior:** since card sizes give separate cards, `renderCards` never asks for a sheet of a card size. Only tests that call a template renderer directly reach the compact branch.
- **Impact:** code that must be maintained and tested without being used.
- **Evidence:** source inspection.
- **Recommended fix:** remove the compact branch and its tests, or state why it is kept.
- **Required verification:** the sheet tests pass without the branch.
- **Correction:** 2026-09-29. The finding was wrong. The QR card of one Shamir share is offered in every size, and in the business size it is printed as a compact sheet, for example with `--card-layout qr --page-size business`. The review followed the path of ordinary cards only. Nothing was removed; the code now names this use in a comment, and `test/card-page-sizes.test.ts`, "prints the QR card of a share as a compact sheet of the business size", proves it.

### Informational observations and recommendations

- The PDF libraries `pdf-lib` and `@pdf-lib/fontkit` had their last release in 2022. No advisory is known, and they only process files that MnemoCode itself creates. Watch for advisories.
- The main function of the command-line program has 174 lines and the sheet layout 115. Splitting them would make them easier to read; no defect follows from their length.

### Assessment and limitations

The reviewed parts keep secrets out of error messages and open no connection. The defects found were a date that was accepted in a form the documents exclude, unclear results and messages at the edge of the card interface, and documents that lagged behind the code.

This record does not cover the Shamir share engine, the recovery checks, image export, the reproducible build, or other platforms. The probe scripts were temporary and are not retained, so the probes can be repeated only through the regression tests named above. The reviewer also wrote the fixes; no second reviewer checked them. This is not a security certification.
