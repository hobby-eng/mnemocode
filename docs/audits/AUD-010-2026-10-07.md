### Informational observations and recommendations

- An import cycle in src/cli only (input.ts, ask.ts, bitcoin-options.ts), with no consequence: every member loads first.
- The IT design now refuses a reference label too long to read at 4.2 pt between 7.2 and 55.5 mm (about 30 characters beside Bytewords), when the cards are drawn; the option accepts 100.
- A word number marked ???? stands for 1000 to 2048 only, since ? is one digit; zero-padded word numbers are read in complete codes but refused beside a ?.
- The menu's tests assert --ask-secrets for five commands; recover-date and sskr-export built by the menu carry it in code but are not asserted.
- colorCards paints --cards with 24-bit backgrounds, a commented exception to the sixteen-colour rule.
- scripts/sign-release.mjs runs gh attestation verify without --signer-workflow or --source-ref (AUD-009's optional recommendation, still open).
- exportSskrCards checks that the target folder is new and renames the staging folder onto it; a folder made in between would be replaced, with no data lost.
- Open architecture item: the restore and export flow of shares (sskr-command.ts restorePhrases and sskr-export) as a host-neutral ShareRestore class, which wallet-tools asked for after this audit's review; planned for the next round.
- The startup self-test now takes about 0.35-0.5 s (5 fresh processes on dist, under load), up from about 0.2 s, with a known answer for every coin and two more features.
- test/card-preview.test.ts draws every design into one PDF and can pass its 120-second timeout on a loaded machine; it takes about 50 s alone.
