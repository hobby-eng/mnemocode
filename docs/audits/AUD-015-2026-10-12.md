### Informational observations and recommendations

**Executable host getters are read again after validation.** Informational robustness scope: changing kind getters selected NoProtection and real MNCL bytes; changing matched getters counted one match after validating boolean false. These require executable host objects, cannot come from JSON or typed native CLI input, and are not a security claim against a dishonest host. Ordinary original reproductions remain fixed. Snapshot validated fields or explicitly document stable-input assumptions.

**Checked checksum resamples a getter-backed input.** An index getter returning 0 during validation and 1 later was read twice and produced false. This proves resampling, not invalid-index acceptance or incorrect checksum math. Copy once and validate/compute the same owned list, or state a stable-input precondition.

**The historical cleanup probe has a narrower oracle than the adjudicated contract.** Its overwrite-after-preflight case passed.
