### Residual AUD-009-FUN003 — Low — Unicode export identities still collide

The new export mark hashes decoy **colors** at `crates/mnemocode-cards/src/content.rs:539`. Unicode colors are assigned by order of first occurrence, independently of the actual code values, at `palette.rs:358`. Different decoy labels with the same repetition pattern therefore produce the same mark. With identical real backup codes, `templates.rs:466` produces the same document ID, and `printed.rs:315` rejects differing codes under the same card number.

The fresh probe creates two Unicode exports from the public all-zero 128-bit phrase, using deterministic bytes `0x11` and `0x42`. Each export reads back exactly on its own. Their decoy labels differ, but their document IDs match. Collection fails with `Different(13)`. The parallel Colors control has distinct IDs and remains readable.

This affects discovery availability; the test does not show substitution of a recovered phrase. Individual exports remain usable. Keeping the original Low severity and finding ID is appropriate.

**Required fix:** derive export identity from the actual ordered decoy labels where labels exist and encoded colors otherwise, or an equivalent identity that distinguishes those contents. Keep the same identity across the sheet and separate cards of one export. Do not suppress genuine conflicting-card errors.

**Required verification:** retain both Colors and Unicode controls, check different random decoy patterns, and cover equivalent SSKR exports and cross-layout grouping.
